Welcome

We are conducting an independent research study on the factors shaping the voting behaviour of first-time voters in India. This survey is intended for individuals who were eligible to vote for the first time in the 2024 Lok Sabha election. It takes approximately 4 minutes. Participation is voluntary. Responses will be used only for academic research and reported in aggregate. By continuing, you confirm that you agree and give consent to participating in this study.
